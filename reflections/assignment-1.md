1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

   During Assignment 1, the first thing I needed to decide was the topic. Once it was confirmed, the brainstorming and planning process went quite smoothly. The main turning point came after Claude generated the first version of the website. Although it seemed to implement the plan, the result did not work smoothly in practice, especially the timeline section.

   I kept giving Claude feedback, rejecting some of its proposals, and explaining what needed to be improved. After several rounds of revision, I finally achieved the result I wanted. Claude also became faster at understanding my intentions, which gave me a deeper understanding of how negative feedback can improve the development process.

2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

   As a software developer, I think it is important to keep my own way of thinking and my own ideas, especially when using AI for vibe coding. It is easy to be led in a different direction and end up with a project that no longer matches the original intention.
   
   At the beginning, Claude assumed that my main idea was to show how different places change at different speeds. What I actually wanted to express was that even materials that are difficult to break down will eventually become part of nature. This difference was not obvious at first, but because I did not clarify the idea early enough, the website, especially the accompanying text, gradually moved away from my original intention.
   
   I need to remember that I am the one using the tool and keep checking whether the core of my own ideas and creativity is still being preserved throughout development.
